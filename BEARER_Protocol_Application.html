BEARER Protocol Election Operations Demo

This prototype demonstrates a fictional election-operations workflow using clearly synthetic data. It does not collect real votes, identities, biometrics, credentials, or cryptographic keys, and it does not claim any real government authorization or certification outcome.

Security and legal disclaimer

Nothing in this repository should be used for live elections, voter registration, identity proofing, key management, ballot delivery, ballot return, tabulation, auditing, or compliance assertions.

All “GovRAMP,” “ISO,” cryptography, audit, and chain-of-custody references are illustrative placeholders.
